What to ask on each call

  1. Can the company name go on a building directory from the first month? No listing here publishes one.
  2. Can the company name go on the office door and a window, not just a road sign? This is never published.
  3. Is the space available now, and what is the shortest term the owner will accept? Ask for month-to-month or 6-month pricing in writing.
  4. For the larger spaces, will the landlord rent one or two rooms on their own, and at what price?
  5. Does the office have its own street-level door, will mail addressed to the suite be accepted, and is anyone on site during business hours?

How this was put together

Business confirmed. Custom Fit Plumbing is on the GMB board in monday.com with its shop at 225 Wayne Ave, Eaton, and the phone number on customfitplumbingllc.com, (937) 340-4441, matches. The site lists water heaters, well and septic pumps and water softeners among its services and names Oxford and Greenville in its service area. The three towns were chosen in the request. Eaton was not treated as already covered, but the recommendation notes where the shop overlaps it.

Town centers. Eaton is centered on the Preble County Courthouse, 101 E Main St; Oxford on the Oxford City Building, 15 S Poplar St; Greenville on the Darke County Courthouse, 504 S Broadway. The centers, offices and competitor addresses were placed with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder, and distances are straight-line miles.

Offices. Small offices within about 5 to 6 miles of each center, found on listing sites (Cityfeet, Showcase, LoopNet, Crexi, Realmo, CommercialCafe, CommercialSearch, OfficeSpace.com, ZoomProspector), broker and property manager pages, Oxford Mainstreet and operator sites, checked November 23 and 24, 2026. Ratings are green only when a listing or the operator says so; nothing was called or visited. Prices are the published monthly rate and may change. Cityfeet, Showcase, Realmo, the Greater Oxford Group site and Oxford Mainstreet would not load for photo capture, so only two offices have a listing photo.

Search demand. Pulled from Google Keyword Planner on November 23 and 24, 2026, for November 2025 to October 2026, Google searches only. Keyword ideas came from seven seed terms (plumber, plumbing, drain cleaning, water heater repair, septic pumping, well pump, water softener), run separately for Eaton, Oxford and Greenville as Google defines each city; Google puts their reach at 24,000, 85,000 and 88,000 people. Each run returned 5,672 ideas, of which 1,044, 1,178 and 1,271 had searches. After the filters on the Search demand page and merging terms Google reports as the same search, 317, 358 and 377 terms were kept. Because the account is not spending, Google gives each keyword a range for each month: the lowest figure counts 10 for a 10 to 100 month and 100 for a 100 to 1,000 month, the middle figure uses Google’s own middle value (50 and 500), and the top uses 100 and 1,000.

Competitors. BBB’s Plumber category, searched by distance from Eaton, Oxford, Greenville and nearby ZIP codes and villages on November 24, 2026, reading up to 15 pages for each search (BBB’s limit). A listing was counted if its name or main BBB category is plumbing and it gives a street address; Custom Fit Plumbing itself was left out. Addresses were placed with the Census geocoder, and a company with several addresses counts once per town. Franchise means Roto-Rooter; multi-location means more than one address on BBB. BBB lists only businesses with a BBB profile, so the counts are a floor; all three towns were counted the same way.

Recommendation. Greenville ranks first on both total searches and searches per plumber within 5 miles. Eaton and Oxford are close to each other on searches per plumber.
